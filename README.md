## Introduction  

The Fikastalle Restaurant Management System simplifies and enhances restaurant operations for users, admins, and staff. It ensures efficient order management, booking, and payment processes.  

---

## Features  

### User-Side Features  

- View the restaurant menu and place orders.  
- Book tables or the entire venue for events.  
- Manage orders and track statuses.  
- Receive exclusive incentives such as vouchers and promos.  

### Admin-Side Features  

- Manage menu items, bookings, orders, and incentives.  
- View detailed reports and monitor restaurant performance.  
- Oversee user accounts and roles.  

### Staff-Side Features  

- Manage order preparation and serving.  
- Toggle item availability.  

---

## User-Side Details  

### Navbar  

- **Non-Registered Users**: Home, Menu, Book, Orders, Login.  
- **Registered Users**: Home, Menu, Book, Orders, Cart, Notifications, Profile, Logout.  

### Footer  

- Displays the logo, contact details, and social media links.  

### Pages and Functionalities  

#### Home  

- Displays banner slides, featured items, and an "About Us" section.  

#### Login Page  

- Login and registration for new users.  

#### Sign Up  

- Requires all fields to be completed.  
- Email verification is mandatory to complete registration.  

#### Menu  

- Search and filter food items.  
- View item details, add to cart, and set preferences.  

#### Cart  

- View and update cart items, including quantities and preferences.  
- Choose dining options, apply discounts, and proceed to checkout.  

#### Orders  

- Track order statuses such as "TO PAY", "PAID", "PREP", "READY", "DONE", etc.  

#### Book  

- **Table Reservation**: Book tables with a refundable reservation fee.  
- **Exclusive Café**: Book the entire venue with custom packages.  

### My Reservations  

- View and manage all reservations, including status and cancellations.  

---

## Admin-Side Details  

### Navbar and Footer  

- **Navbar**: Home, Menu, Landing, Incentives, Bookings, Orders, Accounts, Logout.  
- **Footer**: Displays the logo, contact details, and social links.  

### Functionalities  

#### Home (Dashboard)  

- View daily, weekly, or yearly reports.  
- Manage expenses and announcements.  

#### Menu Management  

- Add, edit, or remove menu items.  
- Manage availability and categories.  

#### Landing Page Management  

- Customize banners, restaurant information, and footer details.  

#### Incentives Management  

- Create and manage vouchers and promo codes.  

#### Bookings Management  

- Approve or reject table and event bookings.  

#### Orders Management  

- Update order statuses and process payments.  

#### Accounts Management  

- Manage user and staff accounts, roles, and security.  

---

## Staff-Side Details  

### Navbar  

- Menu, Orders.  

### Functionalities  

#### Order Management  

- Kitchen: Prepare and mark orders as "READY".  
- Bar: Serve and mark orders as "DONE".  
- Switch order layout positions for efficient workflow.  

#### Menu Management  

- Update item availability and add new items.

## How to Use  

1. Navigate to the website: [fikastalle-cafe.vercel.app](https://fikastalle-cafe.vercel.app).  
2. Register or log in to access full features.  
3. Explore menus, place orders, and manage bookings seamlessly.
